# Invariant Surface: convex-layer output contract

This specification governs the editable browser preview, not the verified runtime. Scandoc Rootsearch is the proposed search for continuity, declared layer violations and unresolved endpoints within convex surface topology. The Surface and Tap instruments remain independent; their proposed common trajectory and core balance are not measured or connected by this preview.

## Frame and scope

- **Screen:** An outward-facing tangent chart for convex-layer relations. It is not a reconstructed Earth surface or a radius-origin search.
- **Core reference:** An opaque spirit-level mark depicts an occluded reference. Optional device orientation moves its dot as a tilt proxy; it does not locate or image the Earth’s core.
- **Altitude:** A manually declared contextual value, kept separate from median correction, curvature fitting and origin inference. No browser altimeter is claimed.
- **Orrery relationship:** Local frames and external perturbations remain distinct. The repository’s Moon/Sun tide model affecting a collider ring does not establish collider activity as a cause of Earth-layer violations.
- **Sphere:** Illustrative local chart only. The quarantined benchmark is not promoted to a canonical organism topology.

Constant spherical curvature does not change merely because a finger moves. The gesture excites the output and traverses a declared chart; it is not a geophysical measurement.

## Synthetic trigger policy

These are editable demonstration thresholds, not calibrated Earth measurements:

- Three displayed chart boundaries at normalised distances `0.36`, `0.64`, `0.90`; only `0.64` is declared closed to crossing.
- A sampled crossing from one side of `0.64` to the other selects sawtooth.
- A direction reversal with cosine below `-0.4`, or a position within `0.04` of a boundary, selects wobble unless a higher-priority crossing occurs.
- A chart endpoint beyond `1.08` is unresolved and silent.
- Priority: unresolved endpoint, closed crossing, ambiguity, continuity.
- These distances are UI chart coordinates, not a fitted physical radius. Unsampled intermediate crossings are not certified.

## Indirect rhythm

First contact starts the local sixteen-step loop and unlocks Web Audio if available and enabled. Gesture outputs seed step `(current_step + 2 + layer_band) mod 16`; two quiet anchor steps support the loop. Contact timing is not recorded or replayed. Repeated touches revise bounded derived cells; they do not accumulate an input archive.

Tempo defaults to 84 BPM with eighth-note steps and a 48–120 BPM range. Output gain is deliberately limited; the slider spans 0–35% of the app’s own gain control, not a measured sound-pressure level. Sound can be muted independently. Stop cancels scheduled sound; Clear destroys the pattern and transient traces.

## Zero-data and lifecycle

There is no gesture stream export, audio recording, media capture, user identity, raw sensor archive or network submission. Two temporary contact values may exist while a drag is active and are dropped on release/cancel. Derived traces are limited to 24 and expire after six seconds; sixteen rhythm cells exist only in this page’s memory.

App-unretained audio is not guaranteed externally unrecordable. Screenshots, operating-system capture or another device may capture perceived output. External font loading remains optional and is not a data-processing service.

Hiding or leaving the page stops sound, clears outputs, disables tilt and discards the in-memory contact state. iPhone orientation requires the user’s explicit permission where the browser requests it; denial or missing readings keeps the symbolic reference.

## Limits

No measured nexus, real Tap transport, actual multi-LLM inference, high-energy-site causation, automatic ergonomic correction, source authentication, full VelocitySplat kernel, physical invariant proof or production integration is claimed. The sound/pad implementation is a bounded interactive output model, not the Python paired-contract engine.
